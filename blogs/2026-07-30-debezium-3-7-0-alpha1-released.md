As summer heats up, so does the pace of innovation in the Debezium community. We’re thrilled to announce Debezium 3.7.0.Alpha1 , the first preview release of the 3.7 series, packed with new features and improvements. Highlights include an incubating Ingres connector, a new ExtendedTimestamp type for high-precision timestamps, and expanded JDBC sink support with CockroachDB, SingleStore, and Starrocks dialects.
