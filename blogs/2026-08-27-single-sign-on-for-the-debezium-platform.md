Identity and Access Management is a highly anticipated feature for the Debezium Platform, slatted for Q4. Until then, we can explore what is currently possible: Single Sign-on, OpenID Connect, and even brokering Entra ID. In this first post, I will walk you through putting a Debezium Platform deployment on Kubernetes behind a single sign-on (SSO) login with Keycloak as an Identity Provider.
