As the leaves start to turn, we’re pleased to announce Debezium 3.7.0.Beta2 , a release that delivers the first official release of the Milvus and SQLite source connectors and the Debezium CLI, alongside new capabilities across the platform, core library, and connectors. Among the highlights, the Debezium Platform gains alerting, the reselect columns post processor gets a RocksDB-backed cache, a new embeddings transformation supports OpenAI models, and a rework of the JMX LastEvent metric rendering delivers improved snapshot throughput.
