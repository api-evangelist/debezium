The Google Summer of Code 2026 program has officially concluded for Debezium, and we are delighted to announce that all our contributors successfully passed their final evaluations! 🎉 Over the past few months, our contributors have worked closely with their mentors and the wider community to design, implement, test, and document new capabilities across the Debezium ecosystem. Their work was not simply an academic exercise or a collection of experimental prototypes.
